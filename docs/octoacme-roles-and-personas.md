# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and feature prioritization
- Work with **Project Managers** on task estimation and schedule planning
- Coordinate with **QA Leads** on testability and quality standards
- Receive technical guidance from **Technical Leads / Architects** on design decisions
- Engage with **Scrum Masters** during sprint ceremonies
- Consult with **UX/Design Leads** on usability implementation

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Align with **Sponsors/Stakeholders** on business priorities and trade-offs
- Define requirements with **Developers** and **Technical Leads** on feasibility
- Partner with **UX/Design Leads** on user research and design validation
- Collaborate with **Project Managers** on timeline and resource planning
- Review quality outcomes with **QA Leads** and impact metrics

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Product Managers** on roadmap alignment and backlog prioritization
- Coordinate with **Developers** and **Technical Leads** on estimates and dependencies
- Work with **Scrum Masters** on agile process adherence and sprint planning
- Escalate issues to **Sponsors/Stakeholders** for decision-making
- Manage communication with **QA Leads** on release readiness
- Coordinate with **DevOps Engineers** on deployment schedules

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach teams on agile practices. They ensure consistent process execution and foster continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and process impediments
- Coach team members on agile practices and self-organization
- Track and report on sprint metrics and velocity
- Protect the team from scope creep and external interruptions

### Goals
- Enable high-performing, self-organizing teams
- Improve delivery predictability and cycle time
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and retrospective facilitation
- One-on-one coaching with team members
- Impediment tracking and escalation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Support **Developers** in self-organization and removing blockers
- Collaborate with **Project Managers** on sprint planning and schedule management
- Work with **Product Managers** on backlog refinement and prioritization clarity
- Coach **Technical Leads** and team on agile practices and team dynamics
- Partner with **QA Leads** on quality integration into sprint workflows
- Support all team members in process adoption and continuous improvement

---

## QA Lead / Quality Engineer

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality strategy, define testing approaches, and ensure acceptance criteria are met. They balance quality gates with delivery velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance)
- Create and maintain test automation frameworks and suites
- Validate acceptance criteria and sign off on features
- Identify quality risks early and propose mitigation
- Mentor developers on testability and quality practices

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality and acceptance standards before release
- Reduce production defects and post-release firefighting
- Automate repetitive testing to improve efficiency

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews and acceptance criteria validation
- Quality metrics reporting
- Defect tracking and triage

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** on testability design and quality standards
- Align with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and feature validation
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on release readiness and test timelines
- Support **DevOps Engineers** in defining smoke tests and production verification
- Work with **Technical Leads** on test architecture and automation frameworks
- Collaborate with **UX/Design Leads** on usability testing and accessibility validation
- Report quality metrics to **Scrum Masters** for sprint health assessment

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business and organizational interests. They approve budgets, timelines, and priorities, and remove organizational barriers to project success.

### Responsibilities
- Define business priorities and strategic alignment
- Approve project charter, budget, and timeline
- Remove organizational blockers and provide executive support
- Champion the project and communicate value to leadership
- Approve major scope and timeline changes

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Minimize organizational impediments
- Maintain executive visibility and support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision gates and approval meetings
- Executive briefings and announcements

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Provide strategic direction to **Product Managers** on business priorities
- Approve plans and timelines from **Project Managers**
- Make decisions on scope and resource trade-offs affecting **Developers** and **Technical Leads**
- Receive escalations from **Project Managers** on critical issues
- Champion and communicate project value to broader organization
- Review outcome metrics from **Product Managers** and **QA Leads**

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural direction, mentor engineers, and ensure solutions align with technical strategy and scalability goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review and mentor on complex technical decisions
- Identify and manage technical debt
- Ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and secure
- Document technical decisions and rationale (ADRs)

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Build systems that scale with business needs
- Mentor engineers and improve engineering practices

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical documentation (ADRs, design specs)
- Code review feedback on complex changes

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Mentor **Developers** on architecture, design patterns, and best practices
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Work with **Project Managers** on technical risk management and dependency planning
- Partner with **QA Leads** on testability and automation strategy
- Support **DevOps Engineers** in designing scalable, deployable architectures
- Coordinate with **UX/Design Leads** on technical constraints for user experience
- Guide **Scrum Masters** on technical impediments and capacity planning

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
Design Leads own the user experience, conduct research, and ensure features are usable and aligned with product vision.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create design specifications, wireframes, and prototypes
- Review features for usability and accessibility
- Collaborate with product and engineering on trade-offs
- Maintain design systems and consistency guidelines

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce usability issues and user support burden
- Build consistent product identity

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- Usability research findings
- Design specifications and component documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Align with **Product Managers** on user research, feature validation, and usability goals
- Collaborate with **Developers** on design implementation and component usability
- Work with **QA Leads** on usability testing and accessibility validation
- Guide **Technical Leads** on UX constraints and design system architecture
- Report usability findings to **Project Managers** for planning and prioritization
- Coordinate with **Scrum Masters** on design review scheduling within sprints
- Receive feedback from **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on strategic design direction

---

## Operations / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and production reliability. They enable rapid, safe delivery to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure and cloud resources
- Ensure secure, reliable production deployments
- Monitor systems and respond to incidents
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe deployments to production
- Maintain system reliability and security
- Reduce manual operational overhead

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and coordination
- Infrastructure and security requirements gathering
- Incident response and post-mortems

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** on CI/CD integration, testing in pipelines, and deployment procedures
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on release schedules and deployment windows
- Collaborate with **QA Leads** on smoke tests, production verification, and post-deployment validation
- Work with **Technical Leads** on infrastructure architecture and scalability planning
- Support **Product Managers** with production metrics and system reliability data
- Align with **Scrum Masters** on infrastructure capacity and sprint planning
- Report system status and incidents to **Project Managers** and **Sponsors/Stakeholders**
